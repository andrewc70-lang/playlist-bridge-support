Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Playlist Bridge for Plex handles information when you use the macOS application.

Last updated: 30 October 2026

At a glance

  • Playlist files are read and processed locally on your Mac.
  • The app connects to the Plex Media Server address that you configure, to communicate with your server and create playlists.
  • Your Plex authentication token is stored in macOS Keychain.
  • The developer does not operate a cloud service that receives your playlist contents or Plex token.

Information used by the app

Playlist files and music paths: Playlist Bridge reads the playlist files and checks the referenced music-file paths that you choose to make available to the app. This processing takes place on your Mac.

Plex connection details: You provide the address of your Plex Media Server and an authentication token. The app uses these details to connect directly to the server you specify. The token is stored in macOS Keychain rather than being written into the playlist files.

Import activity: The app displays import and validation results locally so you can see what happened. The developer does not receive this activity through a Playlist Bridge-operated account or cloud service.

Network connections

Playlist Bridge makes network requests to the Plex server address you configure. Your Plex server may be on your local network or elsewhere, depending on your setup. Your use of Plex is also subject to Plex's own terms and privacy policy.

Data sharing and tracking

Playlist Bridge does not require you to create an account with the app developer. The developer does not sell your playlist contents or Plex authentication token, and does not operate a service for collecting them. This policy does not cover information collected independently by Apple, GitHub Pages or Plex when you use their services.

Data retention and deletion

Your playlist files remain in the locations you selected. You can remove the app's saved Plex credentials using the app's connection/settings controls or macOS Keychain. Uninstalling the app does not necessarily remove credentials stored separately in Keychain; review Keychain Access if you want to remove them.

Children

Playlist Bridge is a general-purpose music utility and is not designed to collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions, please use the project support page on GitHub. Do not include your Plex token or other confidential information in a public issue.

Playlist Bridge is an independent application and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Plex, Inc.
