Help & Setup

Everything you need to connect Playlist Bridge to your Plex Media Server and import your music playlists.

Getting started

  1. Connect to Plex. Open Plex Connection or Settings in Playlist Bridge and enter the address of your Plex Media Server, your music library section and your Plex token.
  2. Choose your playlist folder. Select the folder containing your exported playlist files. Playlist Bridge supports Apple Music XML, M3U and M3U8.
  3. Choose your Plex Music folder. Select the folder that contains the music files represented in your playlists. The paths in the playlists need to correspond to files accessible to your Mac.
  4. Load playlists. Load the folder, select a playlist and review its contents.
  5. Check track paths. Run the path check to identify tracks that Playlist Bridge cannot locate before importing.
  6. Import. Select the playlists you want and use Import to Plex.
Tip: If your music is on an external drive, connect and mount that drive before checking paths or importing.

Finding your Plex token

Your Plex token lets Playlist Bridge authenticate with your own Plex Media Server. Treat it like a password: anyone who has it may be able to access your Plex server as you.

  1. Open Plex Web and sign in to the account associated with your server.
  2. Browse to an item in your Plex library.
  3. Open that item's menu and choose Get Info.
  4. In the information window, choose View XML (the wording or menu location may vary between Plex versions).
  5. Your browser will open an XML page. Look at the address bar for a parameter named X-Plex-Token.
  6. Copy only the token value after X-Plex-Token= and enter it in Playlist Bridge's Plex connection settings.
Keep your token private. Do not post it in screenshots, support requests, public forums or GitHub issues. If you think it has been exposed, sign out/revoke sessions through your Plex account settings and obtain a fresh token.

For further background, see Plex's official token guide.

Supported playlist formats

Apple Music XML

Export playlists from the Music app as XML files, then place the exported playlist files together in a folder for Playlist Bridge to load.

M3U and M3U8

Playlist Bridge can read standard M3U and UTF-8 M3U8 playlist files. These commonly contain file paths pointing to tracks in your music collection.

Playlist Bridge imports playlist references into Plex; it does not copy your music files or move your music library.

Troubleshooting

Plex won't connect

  • Check that Plex Media Server is running.
  • Confirm the server address and port. A typical local address uses port 32400.
  • Check that the token is current and has been copied without extra spaces.
  • In Xcode-distributed or App Store builds, network access must be permitted by the app's sandbox entitlements.

Tracks are missing or unresolved

  • Make sure the external drive containing your music is connected and mounted.
  • Check that the selected Plex Music folder is the correct folder.
  • Confirm the playlist file paths point to the current locations of the music files.
  • For M3U files, relative paths are resolved relative to the playlist file's location.

A playlist already exists

Playlist Bridge protects existing Plex playlists rather than silently overwriting them. Check the playlist name in Plex before importing again.

The import fails

Check your Plex connection, library selection, music folder access and track paths. Try importing one small playlist first to isolate the issue.

Contact support

For help, you can open a support request in the project's GitHub issue tracker. Please describe what happened and include the app version and relevant error message.

Before posting: remove your Plex token, server credentials, private IP details and any personal music-file paths from screenshots or logs.
